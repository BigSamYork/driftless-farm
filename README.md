# The Driftless Farm - Version 2.1

*A Wisconsin Farm Chronicle, 1900–1970.* Released November 15, 2026.

Open **Strum.html** in a modern browser (Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Firefox) to play. No installation, build
step, or internet connection is needed. Keep the files in this folder together.

The **user manual** is [docs/The_Driftless_Farm_User_Manual.docx](docs/The_Driftless_Farm_User_Manual.docx); the
in-game **Almanac** button summarizes the rules.

## New in 2.1 (since 1.0)

- **Realistic pictures.** Every view is redrawn with one sun lighting each object, textured scenery, painted skies and
  distant bluffs, and a painterly finish. The farm folk, cows, and horses blink, swish, and walk; Nels wears his bib
  overalls and straw hat at work.
- **Buildings tab.** The whole home place, with a picture and Build button for every building.
- **Simpler crops.** Wheat, oats, corn, red clover (alfalfa from 1947), and tobacco. The silo fills itself from your corn.
- **Baling Day (from 1947).** Hay is put up loose until you buy one of ten hay balers; then you can drive the baler yourself.
- **Markets.** A Market window in spring, after harvest, and in the winter round (at fall prices), with cheese sold by the pound.
- **Mini-games** fill the picture area: threshing, Baling Day, buzz saw, splitting, and the sawmill.
- **The Coulee Courier** newspaper title page and Ingrid's hand-written letters, in bundled period fonts.

## Saves

Saves are kept in the browser, per folder or web address. Saves from 1.0 load in 2.1; nothing in the save format changed.
Use **Save → Export to file** before moving the game to another folder, browser, or computer, then **Load** the file there.
